# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you represent
the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository's GitHub profile or
security policy. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove,
edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this
code.
